Shelter and Space

Shelter and space.
Space and body.
Body and yoga.
Dance and flow.

The body searches for its place inside an image. A contour appears to describe an edge, but an edge is never only an edge. It may be shelter, horizon, skin, gesture, or the beginning of another image.

To touch the line is to disturb that certainty.

Pulling a contour apart becomes a small act of investigation. The image stretches between what it is and what it might become. A body becomes a landscape. A room opens into a threshold. A curve remembers another curve.

Consciousness and meditation enter through repetition. Look, touch, pull, release. The transition does not arrive through a cut. It must be felt for, as if the next image were already present inside this one.

Another how, why, weird, however.

Have all the days become a search for the how, the why, the weird, and the however?

The days become weeks and, guess what, the questions remain. I continue investigating with my answer bot. It answers, rearranges, proposes, and generates. But every answer opens another contour.

Perhaps the search is not for an answer. Perhaps it is for the shared shape between questions: the line that belongs to two images, two bodies, two thoughts, or two possible worlds at once.
